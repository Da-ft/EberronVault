| Crysteel              |                    | Crysteel or Riedran crysteel is a special material of crystal alloyed with iron. The crystalline substance that forms the basis for crysteel is mined and quarried from the crystal wastes of Xen'drik. Crysteel is used to make excellent weapons. Du'ulora quori, hashalaq quori, and kalaraq quori are vulnerable to made of crysteel. Crysteel is used in the construction of the central core of the hanbalani altas (quori monoliths) that form the basis for Riedra's control over their empire. The interior of the monolith's shell is also coated with a thin layer of crysteel, which allows for the proper flow of psionic energy towards the control crystals at the apex of the monolith. Buildings constructed with a form of crysteel shed a colorful light when charged with energy from a monolith. Riedran bastion cities are built from a mixture of stone, crysteel, and sentira. |
